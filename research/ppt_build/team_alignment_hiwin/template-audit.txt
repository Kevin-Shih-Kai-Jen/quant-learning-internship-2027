Brand and visual rules
- White canvas, black body text, dark blue oversized bumper text, bright blue accents, and pale-blue cards.
- Inter/Helvetica Neue typography as inherited from the source deck.
- Large left-aligned headings; generous whitespace; body copy should stay short and explanatory.
- Page numbers remain in the bottom-right on body slides.
- Existing rounded cards, lines, icons, image strips, and spacing are inherited and not redrawn.

Inherited placeholder audit
- All visible sample titles, lorem ipsum copy, dates, table values, and page numbers on mapped slides are rewritten in place.
- Empty PowerPoint footer placeholders on mapped body slides are explicitly deleted.
- The two off-canvas empty textboxes on source slide 24 are explicitly deleted.
- Decorative icons, cards, timeline lines/dots, and gradient image strips are preserved.

Insertion contract
- No custom overlay layout is permitted.
- Each output slide duplicates one audited source slide.
- All narrative content must fit inherited text, table, or subtitle slots.
- Speaker notes may receive a concise talk track and a [Sources] block.
